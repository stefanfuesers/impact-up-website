Impulses

Impulses are what we share from our work with the world: thoughts, methods, observations – sometimes just a question that won't let us go. From a project, from the literature, from a conversation that set something in motion. We share them because good impact grows when you make it visible and pass it on – and because the good things in this world multiply when many people keep thinking them further. Look around, take what speaks to you, and follow the thought wherever it leads. Resonance with an impulse is the finest of occasions: write to us.

AI Integration

AI Integration at Impact Up is for NGOs and initiatives working on social and ecological issues. First and foremost, we build tools that support your impact orientation and your systemic work, the way both actually happen in your organisation. Every tool connects to your existing processes, to your planning, team meetings, reports and reflections. That way it helps where you already work, and your team uses it itself. We make room for your impact.

What we build with you.

We first look at how you plan, coordinate, report and reflect today. We build the tools onto that. Typical starting points are these.

  • Impact orientation in everyday work

    Theory of Change, Outcomes and indicators become tools you work with in planning and reporting.

  • Monitoring and learning

    Combining data and reports, so that in your reflections you see faster what your work achieves.

  • Systemic work

    Interactions and actors as maps that you develop further together in workshops and team processes.

  • Planning and facilitation

    Preparing retreats, workshops and team meetings that fit your formats.

  • Organising knowledge

    Preparing your documents and experiences so that your team finds what it needs faster.

  • Capacity and guardrails

    Your team learns to use the tools itself, and you decide which data goes into which systems.

The Tool-Lab.

In our Tool-Lab we develop general tools that everyone can use freely. The Mapping Editor makes interactions in a system visible as a map. The Session Builder assembles flows for workshops and sessions from around 190 methods. Both can be connected with your own AI. For your organisation, we build such tools individually, connected to your work processes.

AI for social impact.

We want to use AI for positive impact in society. Used well, it takes routine work off organisations that work on social and ecological issues. That leaves more time for working with people and for the impact they care about.

To do this, we connect the tools to what makes your organisation what it is, to your language, your impact logic and your standards. Judgement and responsibility stay with people.

Behind this is an idea of the future. AI supports us humans in acting more ethically, for a healthy ecosystem Earth, for peace, social participation and democracy. The philosopher Markus Gabriel calls this ethical intelligence. These pages, too, were created in collaboration with AI.

How an offer comes about.

We see ourselves as an organising actor. Depending on your concern, we bring in further people and develop a solution together with you.

So that we can make you a concrete offer, we first clarify your request together. This initial clarification is free of charge and without obligation. Usually we already develop first approaches in an online conversation on how you can take your concern forward in terms of your impact. Only then do we make you an offer. Write to us.

Impact Up Services

Artificial intelligence is developing very fast right now. Still, it will take years before AI is used for everything. Fundamentally, we want AI to work for good impact. For that, we also need to invite AI to do so — and how you can best use AI in your processes is something we want to develop here together with you.

Anchoring

Anchored in your language, your impact logic, your standards.

The decisive question for us is less what these systems can do than what they bind themselves to. In an organisation that means concretely: to its language, its impact logic, its decision-making paths and its standards for what counts as good. That is exactly where we start — we want to support you in integrating AI into your work in such a way that it serves your impact and holds your standards.

The division of labour is clear in this: the sorting work may go to the machine; judgement, responsibility and the moment in the room stay with people. The competence behind this comes out of intensive practice of our own — building workflows with the newest models, ordering knowledge so that the machine can work with it, and checking results against our own standards. This is how we work ourselves: what stands on these pages came into being in collaboration with artificial intelligence.

Stefan has been quick to try out and put to use new AI technologies over the past years; he currently works with Claude Code and knows it best. As always, it is about you finding your own way, and about us walking it together and organising the support. So whatever you have in mind, write to us.

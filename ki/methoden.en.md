# Session Builder method catalogue (English)

190 methods. Format and field meanings: https://impact-up.org/ki/session-builder.md
Full data for every method (steps, notes, when it fits and when not, sources): https://impact-up.org/session-werkstatt/daten.en.js
Each entry: id · type · field · duration in minutes · group size · online · phases · occasions.

## A · planning, goals, steering

**SMART Goals** · `smart-ziele` · werkzeug · 15–45 min · 1–15 people · online: ja · phases: planen, entscheiden · occasions: coaching, strategie, kickoff
Translate vague intentions into specific, measurable, attractive, realistic, and time-bound goals – the classic of goal clarification. Source: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMART_(Projektmanagement)

**LogFrame** · `logframe` · werkzeug · 60–180 min · 2–12 people · online: ja · phases: planen, entscheiden · occasions: strategie, kickoff
A planning matrix from activities through outputs and outcome to impact – with indicators, means of verification, and assumptions. Source: https://tools4dev.org/resources/how-to-write-a-logical-framework-logframe/

**Theory of Change** · `theory-of-change` · werkzeug · 90–240 min · 3–15 people · online: ja · phases: planen, entscheiden · occasions: strategie, lernformat, retro
A narrative model of the causal pathways and assumptions of an intervention – thought backward from the long-term goal. Source: https://www.theoryofchange.org/what-is-theory-of-change/

**Outcome Mapping** · `outcome-mapping` · werkzeug · 120–240 min · 4–15 people · online: ja · phases: planen, reflektieren · occasions: strategie, lernformat
Plan and track impact as observable behavior change in direct partners (boundary partners) rather than as abstract impact. Source: https://www.betterevaluation.org/methods-approaches/approaches/outcome-mapping

**OKR** · `okr` · werkzeug · 60–120 min · 2–12 people · online: ja · phases: planen, entscheiden · occasions: strategie, kickoff, retro
A motivating objective plus 3-5 measurable key results – focus and alignment in short cycles. Source: https://www.whatmatters.com/faqs/okr-meaning-definition-example

**Agile PM (Scrum/iterative)** · `agiles-pm` · rahmenmodell · 15–120 min · 3–10 people · online: ja · phases: planen, verstetigen, reflektieren · occasions: kickoff, strategie
Steer complex undertakings in short learning and delivery loops instead of full planning – with backlog, sprints, dailies, and retrospectives. Source: https://scrumguides.org/

**Ecosystem Mapping** · `ecosystem-mapping` · methode · 60–180 min · 3–12 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, planen · occasions: strategie, netzwerktreffen, kickoff
Make the actors, relationships, and resource flows of a field visible on a shared map. Source: https://servicedesigntools.org/tools/ecosystem-map

**Social Network Analysis** · `social-network-analysis` · methode · 60–180 min · 5–30 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen · occasions: strategie, teamtag
Measure and visualize a network's relationship structures: who is central, who is isolated, where are clusters and bridges? Source: https://www.betterevaluation.org/methods-approaches/methods/social-network-analysis

**Asset-Based Community Development (ABCD)** · `abcd` · methode · 90–240 min · 8–50 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: planen, oeffnen, verstetigen · occasions: beteiligung, kickoff, netzwerktreffen
Map a community's existing strengths instead of deficits – and spark self-organization from within. Source: https://www.nurturedevelopment.org/asset-based-community-development/

**Stakeholder/Actor Mapping** · `stakeholder-mapping` · werkzeug · 45–120 min · 2–15 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, planen · occasions: kickoff, strategie, entscheidungstreffen
Reveal stakeholders' interests and influence on a matrix in order to plan participation and communication deliberately. Source: https://www.improvementservice.org.uk/business-analysis-framework/consider-perspectives/powerinterest-grid

**Community Canvas** · `community-canvas` · methode · 90–240 min · 3–15 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, planen · occasions: kickoff, strategie, netzwerktreffen
Develop a shared picture of a community across three areas (Identity, Experience, Structure) with 17 topic fields. Source: https://community-canvas.org/

**Platform Design Toolkit** · `platform-design-toolkit` · methode · 120–300 min · 3–12 people · online: ja · phases: planen · occasions: strategie, kickoff
Understand and design an ecosystem as a platform: who produces, who consumes, what value is exchanged? Source: https://www.platformdesigntoolkit.com/

**Strategic Doing** · `strategic-doing` · methode · 90–180 min · 4–20 people · online: ja · phases: planen, entscheiden, verstetigen · occasions: netzwerktreffen, kickoff, strategie
Bring loose networks without formal authority into joint action through small commitments and a 30/30 rhythm. Source: https://strategicdoing.net/

**Emergent Strategy (adrienne maree brown)** · `emergent-strategy` · rahmenmodell · 60–180 min · 3–25 people · online: ja · phases: planen, verstetigen, reflektieren · occasions: strategie, retro, dialograum
Strategy as an adaptive, relationship-based practice: fractal experiments amplify what is already emerging. Source: https://adriennemareebrown.net/book/emergent-strategy/

**Backcasting** · `backcasting` · methode · 60–180 min · 4–30 people · online: ja · phases: planen · occasions: strategie, kickoff
Plan backward from a concretely described future picture to milestones and first steps – frees thinking from the status quo. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/methods/backcasting

**Scenario Planning** · `szenario-planung` · methode · 120–360 min · 6–30 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, planen · occasions: strategie
Play through several plausible futures along two critical uncertainties, to make strategies robust against uncertainty. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/blog/scenario-planning/

**RPP Matrix (Reflecting on Peace Practice)** · `rpp-matrix` · werkzeug · 45–120 min · 4–20 people · online: ja · phases: planen, verstehen, reflektieren · occasions: strategie, lernformat, retro
A strategy check for peace and change programs: all activities get placed in four quadrants (many people vs. key people x individual vs. socio-political change). Reveals where the program has impact - and which field remains empty. Source: https://www.cdacollaborative.org/publication/reflecting-on-peace-practice-rpp-basics-a-resource-manual/

**Future Vision as Film Script** · `zukunftsvision-filmskript` · methode · 30–90 min · 4–30 people · online: ja · phases: planen, oeffnen, ankommen · occasions: strategie, kickoff, dialograum
Narrative visioning work: the group describes the desired state in three years as a sensory scene ("What do you see, what do you hear, who's there?") instead of a list of goals. Miracle-question logic for strategy and kickoff processes.

## B · organisation, consulting

**Systemic Organizational Consulting** · `systemische-organisationsberatung` · rahmenmodell · 60–240 min · 1–20 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, verstehen, vertiefen · occasions: coaching, konfliktklaerung, strategie
Understand organizations as social systems and accompany them through self-directed change – via hypotheses, questions, and settings. Source: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systemische_Organisationsberatung

**Process Consultation (Schein)** · `prozessberatung-schein` · haltung · 30–120 min · 1–15 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, verstehen, vertiefen · occasions: coaching, konfliktklaerung, schwieriges_gespraech
Shape the client's working and relationship process instead of delivering content – with humble inquiry and a helping relationship. Source: https://www.kantelbergs.de/prinzipien-prozessberatung-schein/

**Lewin (3 Phases)** · `lewin` · rahmenmodell · 45–120 min · 3–20 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, planen · occasions: strategie, lernformat, retro
Structure change in three phases – unfreeze, change, refreeze – with force-field analysis as a practical building block. Source: https://projekte-leicht-gemacht.de/blog/business-wissen/das-3-phasen-modell-lewin/

**Glasl (Organizational Phases & Escalation)** · `glasl-org-phasen` · rahmenmodell · 60–120 min · 3–20 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen · occasions: strategie, lernformat, retro
Determine an organization's stage of maturity (pioneer, differentiation, integration, association phase) and interpret crises as normal developmental steps. Source: https://www.fritz.tips/entwicklungsphasen-einer-organisation-nach-friedrich-glasl

**Kotter (8 Steps)** · `kotter-8-stufen` · rahmenmodell · 60–150 min · 3–20 people · online: ja · phases: planen · occasions: strategie, kickoff, lernformat
Plan large-scale organizational change along eight steps – from urgency through guiding coalition to anchoring. Source: https://www.kotterinc.com/methodology/8-steps/

**Theory U / Laloux** · `theory-u-laloux` · rahmenmodell · 90–360 min · 5–40 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: verstehen, oeffnen, vertiefen · occasions: strategie, dialograum, lernformat
Lead profound change from the emerging future potential (presencing) – complemented by Laloux's images of evolutionary, self-organized organizations. Source: https://www.presencing.org/theoryu

**Contracting & Clarifying the Assignment** · `auftragsklaerung-kontrakt` · werkzeug · 20–60 min · 1–10 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: coaching, kickoff, schwieriges_gespraech
Before starting work, clarify who wants what from whom – occasion, concern, and assignment together form the contract. The foundation of every consulting and facilitation process. Source: https://istob-zentrum.de/systemischer-spickzettel-auftragsklaerung/

**Client Types (de Shazer)** · `kundentypen-de-shazer` · werkzeug · 10–30 min · 1–5 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, verstehen · occasions: coaching, schwieriges_gespraech
Recognize the client's relationship stance – visitor, complainant, or customer – and dose interventions accordingly. Source: https://consiglio.at/die-drei-kliententypen-in-der-beratung-nach-steve-de-shazer/

**Systemic Questioning Techniques** · `systemische-fragetechniken` · werkzeug · 10–60 min · 1–15 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, vertiefen · occasions: coaching, konfliktklaerung, schwieriges_gespraech, teamtag
Use circular, scaling, and hypothetical questions to generate new perspectives, distinctions, and solution images within the system. Source: https://karrierebibel.de/systemisches-fragen/

**Systemic Loop** · `systemische-schleife` · haltung · 15–60 min · 1–10 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, vertiefen, reflektieren · occasions: coaching, konfliktklaerung, lernformat
An internal navigation aid for consultants: observe, form hypotheses, intervene, observe again – in a cycle. Source: https://systemischesnetzwerk.de/die-systemische-schleife-als-navigationshilfe/

**Hypothesis Formation (Milan School)** · `hypothesenbildung` · werkzeug · 15–45 min · 1–8 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen · occasions: coaching, konfliktklaerung, lernformat
Develop tentative circular explanatory offers about the function of behavior – usefulness counts, not truth. Source: https://www.carl-auer.de/magazin/systemisches-lexikon/hypothetisieren

**9-Field Model & PUMA** · `neun-felder-puma` · werkzeug · 30–90 min · 1–6 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, verstehen · occasions: coaching, schwieriges_gespraech
Sharpen the concern and steer where a conversation stands between problem, resource, and solution space across three time perspectives. Source: https://mediator.sweti.de/post/9-felder-modell/

## C · group, team

**Tuckman (Team Phases)** · `tuckman` · rahmenmodell · 30–90 min · 3–20 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, reflektieren · occasions: teamtag, retro, lernformat
Interpret team dynamics as normal developmental phases – forming, storming, norming, performing, adjourning – and choose fitting support. Source: https://mychange.solutions/toolbox/teamwork/phasen-der-teamentwicklung

**Iceberg Model** · `eisbergmodell` · rahmenmodell · 30–90 min · 2–20 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, konflikt · occasions: konfliktklaerung, teamtag, lernformat
Make visible that hidden relationship and emotional dynamics (~80%) drive the visible behavior on the content level. Source: https://projekte-leicht-gemacht.de/blog/softskills/kommunikation/das-eisbergmodell/

**Rank Dynamics (Schindler)** · `rangdynamik-schindler` · rahmenmodell · 45–90 min · 4–16 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen · occasions: teamtag, lernformat, retro
Make informal leadership and resistance roles in groups (alpha, beta, gamma, omega) visible and discussable. Source: https://wirtrainieren.de/werkzeugkoffer/rangdynamik-schindler/

**Team Roles (Belbin)** · `teamrollen-belbin` · rahmenmodell · 60–150 min · 3–15 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, verstetigen · occasions: kickoff, teamtag, lernformat
Make functional role diversity in the team visible – nine roles in the clusters thinking, doing, relating – and balance out gaps. Source: https://www.belbin.com/about/belbin-team-roles

**Process Curves** · `prozesskurven` · rahmenmodell · 20–60 min · 4–25 people · online: ja · phases: vertiefen, reflektieren · occasions: retro, teamtag, lernformat
Make the energetic course of a group process visible as a curve and de-dramatize dips as normal working phases. Source: https://www.die-bonn.de/wb/2015-gruppendynamik-01.pdf

**Factors of Successful Teamwork** · `faktoren-teamarbeit` · rahmenmodell · 60–120 min · 3–15 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, verstetigen, reflektieren · occasions: teamtag, retro, kickoff
Diagnose team quality using the five factors from Google's Project Aristotle – psychological safety, dependability, structure & clarity, meaning, impact. Source: https://rework.withgoogle.com/intl/en/guides/understand-team-effectiveness

**Open Space** · `open-space` · methode · 180–480 min · 15–500 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: oeffnen, vertiefen · occasions: beteiligung, strategie, netzwerktreffen
Large-group format in which participants set their own agenda – with marketplace, the Law of Two Feet, and self-organized sessions. Source: https://openspaceworld.org/wp2/what-is/

**World Café** · `world-cafe` · methode · 90–180 min · 12–200 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: oeffnen · occasions: beteiligung, strategie, netzwerktreffen
Connect the perspectives of many people through rotating table conversations and surface collective patterns. Source: https://theworldcafe.com/key-concepts-resources/world-cafe-method/

**Appreciative Inquiry** · `appreciative-inquiry` · methode · 90–480 min · 6–300 people · online: ja · phases: oeffnen, vertiefen, planen · occasions: kickoff, strategie, teamtag, beteiligung
Develop change from a system's strengths and best experiences – in the 4-D cycle Discovery, Dream, Design, Destiny. Source: https://aicommons.champlain.edu/educational-material/4d-appreciative-inquiry-model/

**Future Search** · `future-search` · methode · 480–1200 min · 40–80 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: oeffnen, planen, entscheiden · occasions: strategie, beteiligung, netzwerktreffen
Bring all stakeholders of a system to a shared future image and common action on common ground – a large-scale format over several days. Source: https://futuresearch.net/about/methodology/

**Liberating Structures** · `liberating-structures` · methode · 10–60 min · 4–200 people · online: ja · phases: oeffnen, vertiefen, entscheiden · occasions: beteiligung, lernformat, regeltermin
33 precise micro-structures (e.g. 1-2-4-All, TRIZ, Troika Consulting) that include all voices and replace conventional meeting patterns. Source: https://www.liberatingstructures.com/

**Fishbowl** · `fishbowl` · methode · 30–90 min · 12–100 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: oeffnen, vertiefen · occasions: dialograum, beteiligung, lernformat
Inner and outer circle: a focused, in-depth discussion in large groups that anyone can join via the empty chair. Source: https://www.methodenkartei.uni-oldenburg.de/methode/fishbowl-diskussion/

**Barcamp/Unconference** · `barcamp` · methode · 240–480 min · 20–300 people · online: ja · phases: oeffnen · occasions: netzwerktreffen, lernformat, beteiligung
Participants design the conference program themselves in the morning and share knowledge as equals – with a session board and the Law of Two Feet. Source: https://wb-web.de/material/interaktion/barcamp-planen-und-durchfuehren.html

**Citizens' Assembly/Planning Cell** · `buergerrat-planungszelle` · methode · 480–2400 min · 25–160 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: oeffnen, entscheiden · occasions: beteiligung, entscheidungstreffen, lernformat
Randomly selected citizens deliberate on a political question with informed input and give recommendations as a citizens' report. Source: https://www.buergergesellschaft.de/mitentscheiden/methoden-verfahren/methoden-und-verfahren-der-buergerbeteiligung-von-a-bis-z/planungszelle-buergergutachten/methodenbeschreibung

**Palodium (IPG)** · `palodium` · methode · 60–180 min · 20–200 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: oeffnen, vertiefen · occasions: beteiligung, lernformat, netzwerktreffen
Interweave panel discussion and small-group palaver ("Greek coffeehouse") so large groups are actively involved rather than just listening. Source: https://gemeinsamdigital.berlin.de/documents/48/GDB_Materialien_fur_das_Handbuch_-_Arbeitsanweisung_A3___Palodium.pdf

**New Games** · `new-games` · methode · 10–45 min · 6–100 people · online: nein · phases: ankommen, vertiefen · occasions: kickoff, teamtag, lernformat
Movement games with no winners or losers – "play hard, play fair, nobody hurt" – build contact and a sense of community. Source: https://wirkstatt.com/newgames

**Initiative Tasks** · `initiativaufgaben` · methode · 45–120 min · 6–16 people · online: nein · phases: vertiefen, reflektieren · occasions: teamtag, kickoff, lernformat
Tasks like the spider web, acid pond, or tower building that can only be solved through everyone's cooperation – making team processes tangible. Source: https://itps.uni-wuppertal.de/fileadmin/itps/Wastl/EP-Aktiv-04.pdf

**Trust Exercises** · `trust-uebungen` · methode · 15–60 min · 2–30 people · online: nein · phases: vertiefen · occasions: teamtag, lernformat
Let go of control and experience trust physically – from blind leading to the trust fall in a tight circle. Source: https://www.praxis-jugendarbeit.de/spielesammlung/spiele-vertrauen.html

**Betzavta Dilemmas** · `betzavta-dilemmata` · methode · 60–180 min · 8–25 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: vertiefen, konflikt, reflektieren · occasions: lernformat, dialograum, teamtag
Make democratic principles tangible through experienced dilemmas instead of instruction – both claims, my freedom and that of others, also exist within me. Source: https://www.cap-lmu.de/akademie/schwerpunkte/betzavta-miteinander/

**(Iterated) Prisoner's Dilemma** · `gefangenendilemma` · methode · 30–90 min · 4–40 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, vertiefen · occasions: lernformat, teamtag, dialograum
Make the tension between self-interest and cooperation tangible through game theory and derive conditions for trust. Source: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/prisoner-dilemma/

**Weather Report (Check-in)** · `wetterbericht-check-in` · werkzeug · 5–15 min · 3–30 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: regeltermin, teamtag, coaching
Describe your inner state as a weather forecast – a quick check-in without pressure to explain, the metaphor protects. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/methods/weather-check-in-obyout

**One-Word Check-in** · `ein-wort-check-in` · werkzeug · 3–10 min · 3–40 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, reflektieren · occasions: regeltermin, teamtag, retro
Arrive with a single word – make all voices heard in the shortest time and get a mood picture. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/methods/one-word-method

**Mood Scale 1–10** · `stimmungsskala` · werkzeug · 3–10 min · 3–100 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, reflektieren · occasions: regeltermin, entscheidungstreffen, teamtag, krisensitzung
Quickly quantify the group's energy or agreement – by show of hands, card, spatial line-up, or chat – and make it actionable. Source: https://www.selbstlernen.net/aufstellung-und-skalenabfrage/

**1-2-4-All** · `1-2-4-all` · methode · 12–25 min · 4–200 people · online: ja · phases: oeffnen, vertiefen · occasions: beteiligung, strategie, retro
Activate everyone at once: think alone in silence, then in pairs, then in fours – and carry only the best ideas to the plenary (Liberating Structures). Source: https://www.liberatingstructures.com/1-1-2-4-all/

**Think-Pair-Share** · `think-pair-share` · methode · 5–20 min · 4–100 people · online: ja · phases: oeffnen, vertiefen · occasions: lernformat, regeltermin, retro
Individual thinking, pair exchange, plenary: secure everyone's participation and raise contribution quality through forethought. Source: https://www.methodenkartei.uni-oldenburg.de/methode/think-pair-share/

**Lightning Round (Blitzlicht)** · `blitzlicht` · werkzeug · 5–15 min · 3–15 people · online: ja · phases: reflektieren · occasions: regeltermin, retro, teamtag, krisensitzung
Going around the circle, one sentence per person, no comments – a quick, unfiltered snapshot of the whole group's mood or opinion. Source: https://www.methodenkartei.uni-oldenburg.de/methode/blitzlicht/

**Pair Interview (Icebreaker)** · `paarinterview` · methode · 15–40 min · 6–40 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: kickoff, netzwerktreffen, teamtag
Interview each other and introduce your partner to the plenary – contact through stories instead of self-presentation. Source: https://www.methodenkartei.uni-oldenburg.de/methode/partnerinterview/

**Warp Speed (Energizer)** · `warp-speed` · methode · 5–15 min · 6–25 people · online: nein · phases: ankommen · occasions: teamtag, lernformat, kickoff
Complete the ball-passing order in a circle faster and faster – activates the group in minutes and makes creative problem-solving beyond habitual assumptions tangible. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/methods/warp-speed

**Counting Circle (Energizer)** · `zaehlkreis` · methode · 5–10 min · 5–25 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: ankommen · occasions: teamtag, lernformat
Count together to 20 with no agreed order – sharpens attention, listening, and shared timing. Source: https://dramaresource.com/count-to-20/

**Feelings & Needs Cards (NVC mini)** · `gefuehls-beduerfnis-karten` · werkzeug · 10–30 min · 2–25 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, vertiefen, konflikt · occasions: konfliktklaerung, teamtag, coaching
Cards with feelings and needs words lower the threshold for talking about one's own situation – naming without having to justify. Source: https://www.cnvc.org/store/feelings-and-needs-inventory

**Self-Empathy Pause (NVC mini)** · `selbstempathie-pause` · werkzeug · 2–10 min · 1–30 people · online: ja · phases: konflikt, vertiefen · occasions: konfliktklaerung, schwieriges_gespraech, coaching
Clarify inwardly in 2-3 minutes what I feel and need – self-regulation in the middle of a heated session. Source: https://www.fachverband-gfk.org/der-prozess-der-selbstempathie

**Grounding / Short Body Scan (Embodiment)** · `erdung-bodyscan` · werkzeug · 3–15 min · 1–100 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: teamtag, lernformat, coaching
Bring the group from the head into body awareness: attention travels through the body, feeling contact surfaces, arriving. Source: https://www.mindful.org/beginners-body-scan-meditation/

**Pair Interview with Introduction** · `paarinterview-mit-vorstellung` · methode · 15–30 min · 6–40 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: kickoff, netzwerktreffen, teamtag
Two people interview each other (5 min per direction) and then introduce their partner in plenary. A classic for getting acquainted with built-in listening practice. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/blog/icebreaker-games/

**Appreciative Interviews (Liberating Structures)** · `appreciative-interviews` · methode · 40–60 min · 8–100 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, oeffnen · occasions: kickoff, teamtag, strategie
Pair interviews about a concrete success story; in groups of four, each person's partner's story is retold and searched for the conditions of success. Source: https://www.liberatingstructures.com/5-appreciative-interviews-ai/

**Impromptu Networking (Liberating Structures)** · `impromptu-networking` · methode · 15–20 min · 8–200 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: kickoff, netzwerktreffen, beteiligung
Three quick pair conversations of 4–5 minutes each around a double question, with a partner switch after each round. Everyone talks to three people within the first 15 minutes. Source: https://www.liberatingstructures.com/impromptu-networking

**Three Things in Common** · `drei-dinge-gemeinsam` · methode · 5–15 min · 4–100 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: kickoff, netzwerktreffen, teamtag
Pairs or small groups have 3-5 minutes to find three surprising things they have in common - the obvious ones like "we both work here" don't count. Source: https://icebreaker-games.org/three-things-in-common

**Low-Tech Social Network** · `low-tech-social-network` · methode · 20–40 min · 10–100 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: ankommen · occasions: netzwerktreffen, kickoff, beteiligung
Everyone draws themselves as an "avatar" on a card, sticks it to the wall, and draws connecting lines to people they know - an analog network map of the group emerges. Source: https://gamestorming.com/low-tech-social-network/

**Trust Walk (Leading Blind)** · `blind-fuehren-trust-walk` · methode · 15–30 min · 4–30 people · online: nein · phases: oeffnen, vertiefen · occasions: teamtag, lernformat
One person closes their eyes, their partner leads them safely through the room - first by hand, then by voice only. Trust becomes a physical experience: giving up control, taking on responsibility. Source: https://www.seedsforchange.org.uk/tools

**Human Knot** · `human-knot` · methode · 10–20 min · 7–30 people · online: nein · phases: ankommen, oeffnen · occasions: kickoff, teamtag
The group stands in a circle, everyone reaches across and grabs two strangers' hands - then the group untangles itself back into a circle without letting go. A cooperation task with no materials at all. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/methods/human-knot-zmabvk

**Marshmallow Challenge (short tower-building variant)** · `marshmallow-challenge` · methode · 30–45 min · 8–60 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: ankommen, oeffnen · occasions: kickoff, teamtag, lernformat

Harvest collective knowledge from large-group processes and make it usable beyond the event itself. Source: https://www.chriscorrigan.com/parkinglot/the-art-of-harvesting/

Capture impact through significant stories of change rather than only through predefined indicators. Source: https://www.betterevaluation.org/methods-approaches/approaches/most-significant-change

**Mini Body Scan Seated** · `mini-bodyscan-im-sitzen` · methode · 3–7 min · 1–100 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: lernformat, teamtag, regeltermin
A guided attention journey through the body (feet, legs, torso, shoulders, face) while seated on a chair. Brings the group out of their heads and into perception, and marks transitions between thinking blocks. Source: https://ggia.berkeley.edu/practice/body_scan_meditation

**Physiological Sigh** · `physiological-sigh` · methode · 2–5 min · 1–200 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: konfliktklaerung, schwieriges_gespraech, entscheidungstreffen, krisensitzung
Inhale twice in a row through the nose (deep, then a short top-up), then exhale slowly through the mouth. The fastest known single technique against acute tension – works after just 1–3 repetitions. Source: https://med.stanford.edu/news/insights/2023/02/cyclic-sighing-can-help-breathe-away-anxiety.html

**Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)** · `box-breathing` · methode · 2–4 min · 1–200 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: entscheidungstreffen, konfliktklaerung, kickoff
Breathing in a square: 4 seconds inhale, 4 hold, 4 exhale, 4 hold. Known as a Navy SEALs training method – which makes it instantly relatable for method-skeptical professional groups. Source: https://health.clevelandclinic.org/box-breathing-benefits

**4-6 Breathing (Extended Exhale)** · `vier-sechs-atmung` · methode · 2–5 min · 1–200 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: konfliktklaerung, schwieriges_gespraech, teamtag
Inhale for 4 seconds, exhale for 6 – the extended exhale activates the parasympathetic nervous system. The simplest breathing technique there is: no holding, no counting acrobatics. Source: https://stanmed.stanford.edu/cyclic-sighing-stress-relief/

**Shoulder-Neck Reset at the Desk** · `schulter-nacken-reset` · methode · 2–4 min · 1–500 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: regeltermin, lernformat, teamtag
Three short release moves for shoulders, neck and wrists – seated, without standing up. The go-to format for online meetings and sitting-heavy work sessions. Source: https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/adult-health/in-depth/office-stretches/art-20046041

**Stand Up & Stretch Between Blocks** · `aufstehen-und-strecken` · methode · 2–3 min · 2–500 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: lernformat, teamtag, regeltermin
Everyone stands up, stretches upward, to the side, lets their arms drop – 2 minutes of standing stretch as a fixed block-change marker. Source: https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/adult-health/in-depth/office-stretches/art-20046041

**Walk & Talk (Walking as a Thinking Break)** · `walk-and-talk` · methode · 10–20 min · 4–60 people · online: eingeschraenkt · phases: verstehen, reflektieren · occasions: strategie, teamtag, lernformat, dialograum
Pair conversations while walking instead of at the table – 10–15 minutes outside or in the hallway, with a clear guiding question. Walking demonstrably boosts divergent thinking (about 60% more creative answers, Stanford study). Source: https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2014/04/walking-vs-sitting-042414

**5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Exercise (Sensory Anchor)** · `erdung-5-4-3-2-1` · methode · 2–4 min · 1–100 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, konflikt · occasions: konfliktklaerung, schwieriges_gespraech, coaching
Name in sequence: 5 things I see – 4 I feel – 3 I hear – 2 I smell – 1 I taste. A standard technique to move out of rumination or agitation into the here and now. Source: https://www.healthline.com/health/anxiety/5-4-3-2-1-grounding-technique-for-anxiety

**Brief Silence with a Breath Anchor** · `stille-mit-atem-anker` · methode · 1–3 min · 2–200 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, entscheiden · occasions: entscheidungstreffen, konfliktklaerung, dialograum
One to three minutes of shared silence; those who want to can use their own breath as an attention anchor. The most minimalist of all practices – and often the most effective one before decisions. Source: https://ggia.berkeley.edu/practice/mindful_breathing

## G · meeting craft, online facilitation

**Agenda Design with Questions** · `agenda-design-mit-fragen` · werkzeug · 10–30 min · 2–20 people · online: ja · phases: planen · occasions: regeltermin, entscheidungstreffen, kickoff, krisensitzung
Agenda items are formulated as questions to be answered instead of keywords. Each item comes with a purpose (inform / gather input / decide), a time slot, and a responsible person. Source: https://hbr.org/2015/03/how-to-design-an-agenda-for-an-effective-meeting

**Check-in / Check-out (Short Meeting Form)** · `check-in-check-out` · werkzeug · 3–15 min · 2–25 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, reflektieren · occasions: regeltermin, teamtag, retro
A deliberate opening and closing of a meeting: each person briefly says how they're arriving or what they're leaving with. Makes every voice heard once before the substantive work begins. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/methods/check-in-check-out

**Lean Coffee** · `lean-coffee` · methode · 30–60 min · 4–12 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, vertiefen · occasions: netzwerktreffen, regeltermin, retro, krisensitzung
A structured meeting without a prepared agenda: participants propose topics, prioritize by vote, and work through them in timeboxes. A democratically generated agenda in real time. Source: https://leancoffee.org/

**ELMO (Enough, Let's Move On)** · `elmo` · werkzeug · 1–2 min · 3–30 people · online: ja · phases: vertiefen · occasions: regeltermin, entscheidungstreffen
An agreed-upon signal that anyone in the meeting can use to show that a discussion has passed its productive point – by calling out, hand signal, or card. Source: https://www.facilitator.school/glossary/elmo

**Parking Lot** · `parkplatz` · werkzeug · 5–10 min · 3–30 people · online: ja · phases: vertiefen, verstetigen · occasions: regeltermin, strategie, entscheidungstreffen
A visible topic reservoir (flipchart, board area) for points that matter but don't belong to the current agenda. Honors the contribution and protects focus. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/methods/parking-lot

**Who-Does-What-By-When (3W Decision Anchoring)** · `wer-macht-was-bis-wann` · werkzeug · 5–10 min · 2–20 people · online: ja · phases: entscheiden, verstetigen · occasions: regeltermin, entscheidungstreffen, strategie, krisensitzung
Every agreement is captured before the meeting ends as a one-sentence action item: one responsible person (Who), one task with a verb (What), one concrete date (By When). Source: https://www.lucidmeetings.com/glossary/action-item

**Delegation Poker** · `delegation-poker` · methode · 45–90 min · 3–7 people · online: ja · phases: planen, entscheiden · occasions: kickoff, teamtag, entscheidungstreffen
A card game (Management 3.0) that helps team and leadership clarify, for each decision area, which of the seven delegation levels (Tell, Sell, Consult, Agree, Advise, Inquire, Delegate) a decision sits at. Source: https://management30.com/practice/delegation-poker/

**Silent Start / 6-Pager (Memo Reading)** · `silent-start-6-pager` · methode · 30–60 min · 3–15 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, entscheiden · occasions: entscheidungstreffen, strategie
The meeting begins with silent reading: instead of a presentation, the person raising the topic writes a narrative memo in advance (max. six pages); everyone reads silently for the first 10-30 minutes and takes notes. Source: https://www.asyncagile.org/method-stack/6-page-memo

**Timeboxing** · `timeboxing` · werkzeug · 0–5 min · 2–30 people · online: ja · phases: planen, vertiefen · occasions: regeltermin, entscheidungstreffen, strategie, krisensitzung
Every agenda item gets a fixed time window that runs visibly and is held consistently. The time limit creates focus and protects later items. Source: https://asana.com/resources/what-is-timeboxing

**ROTI (Return on Time Invested)** · `roti` · werkzeug · 2–5 min · 3–30 people · online: ja · phases: reflektieren · occasions: regeltermin, retro
Instant feedback at the end of a meeting: everyone rates on a 1-5 scale whether the time invested was worth it. Makes meeting quality measurable and improvable. Source: https://www.lucidmeetings.com/glossary/roti

**Consent (Sociocracy, Short Form)** · `konsent` · methode · 15–45 min · 3–12 people · online: ja · phases: entscheiden · occasions: entscheidungstreffen, regeltermin, teamtag
A sociocratic decision-making process: a proposal is considered accepted if no one has a serious, well-reasoned objection – the question asked is "Does anything speak seriously against this?" instead of "Are you in favor?" Source: https://www.soziokratie.org/konsentmoderation/

**Systemic Consensing (Resistance Polling)** · `systemisches-konsensieren` · methode · 20–60 min · 4–50 people · online: ja · phases: entscheiden · occasions: entscheidungstreffen, konfliktklaerung, beteiligung
A decision process that measures resistance instead of approval: everyone rates each option with 0-10 resistance points; the option with the lowest total resistance is chosen – the least conflictual solution. Source: https://sk-prinzip.eu/methode/

**Dot Voting (with Variants)** · `dot-voting` · werkzeug · 5–15 min · 3–50 people · online: ja · phases: entscheiden, oeffnen · occasions: retro, strategie, beteiligung
Fast prioritization: each person gets a fixed number of sticky dots (or digital votes) and distributes them across the options on display; the density of dots shows the group's preference. Source: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/dot-voting/

**Fist to Five** · `fist-to-five` · werkzeug · 2–10 min · 3–30 people · online: ja · phases: entscheiden · occasions: entscheidungstreffen, regeltermin, krisensitzung
A fast consensus check via hand signal: fist (veto/no) to five fingers (full enthusiasm) show everyone's level of agreement with a proposal at the same time. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/methods/fist-to-five

**Gradients of Agreement** · `gradients-of-agreement` · werkzeug · 10–25 min · 4–30 people · online: ja · phases: entscheiden, vertiefen · occasions: entscheidungstreffen, strategie, konfliktklaerung
An eight-level agreement scale by Sam Kaner (from "fully in favor" to "block") that lets group members express their stance on a proposal in a differentiated way instead of just yes/no. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/methods/gradients-of-agreement

**Advice Process** · `advice-process` · methode · 30–120 min · 2–15 people · online: ja · phases: entscheiden, verstetigen · occasions: entscheidungstreffen, teamtag
A decentralized decision principle (Frédéric Laloux): anyone may make any decision but must first seek advice – from those affected and from those with expertise. The advice flows in, but the decision is made by the individual. Source: https://reinventingorganizationswiki.com/en/theory/decision-making/

**Online Energizer: Scavenger Hunt** · `online-energizer-scavenger-hunt` · methode · 3–7 min · 3–30 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: netzwerktreffen, lernformat
A movement energizer for video calls: everyone quickly fetches an object matching a prompt ("something blue", "something important to you") and shows it on camera. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/blog/online-energizers/

**Online Energizer: Stretch & Shake** · `online-energizer-stretch-shake` · methode · 2–5 min · 2–100 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, vertiefen · occasions: lernformat
A guided 2-3-minute movement break in a video call: standing up together, stretching, rolling shoulders, shaking out – facilitation demonstrates, everyone joins in. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/blog/online-energizers/

**Online Energizer: Virtual Wave / Row Impulse** · `online-energizer-virtual-wave` · methode · 3–5 min · 5–25 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: netzwerktreffen, lernformat
The group sends a "wave" through the video tiles: in an agreed order, everyone in turn makes the same short movement or gesture. Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/blog/online-energizers/

**Breakout Design (small groups online)** · `breakout-design` · werkzeug · 10–30 min · 6–100 people · online: ja · phases: oeffnen, vertiefen · occasions: beteiligung, lernformat, netzwerktreffen
Methodical design of breakout rooms: clear task, appropriate group size, roles, and harvest format are set before the split – the small group is the most important place for real participation online. Source: https://facilitationfirst.com/virtual-breakout-room-best-practices/

**Digital Whiteboard Work (methodical)** · `digitales-whiteboard-arbeiten` · werkzeug · 10–60 min · 3–30 people · online: ja · phases: verstehen, vertiefen, planen · occasions: strategie, lernformat, beteiligung
Methodical ground rules for collaborative work on digital whiteboards (Miro, Mural, FigJam, etc.): prepared structure, tech onboarding, silent parallel writing, and clear navigation instead of free-for-all chaos. Source: https://miro.com/virtual-workshops/guide-to-workshop-facilitation/

**Online Check-in Variants** · `online-check-in-varianten` · werkzeug · 3–10 min · 3–100 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen · occasions: netzwerktreffen, lernformat
Check-in formats adapted to the online space: chat waterfall (everyone types, sends simultaneously on cue), emoji/GIF mood picture, scale via reaction, photo check-in ("show your desk object"). Source: https://www.sessionlab.com/library/check-in

**Camera & Energy Hygiene (long online meetings)** · `kamera-energie-hygiene` · rahmenmodell · 0–10 min · 2–100 people · online: ja · phases: ankommen, vertiefen, verstetigen · occasions: lernformat, netzwerktreffen
Design rules against video-call exhaustion ("Zoom fatigue"), derived from Stanford research (Bailenson): hide self-view, audio-only phases, freedom of movement, and real breaks built in. Source: https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2021/02/four-causes-zoom-fatigue-solutions

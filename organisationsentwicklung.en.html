Systemic Organisational Development

Systemic Organisational Development at Impact Up accompanies teams, organisations and networks in their development. We work as process consultants. You are the experts on your organisation, and we shape with you the process in which you find your own solutions. We are especially familiar with Impact Networks, social movements and social enterprises, in other words settings where people want to move something in society together. Throughout, we keep in view what impact you want to achieve with your work.

What we accompany.

We start from your concern and choose the form that fits it. Often it is one or several of these topics.

  • Team development

    Teams clarify how they work together, what their tasks are and what their shared mandate is.

  • Coaching in organisational and project contexts

    Individuals and leadership teams reflect on their role, their decisions and difficult phases, also over longer periods.

  • Strategy development

    Together you develop where your organisation wants to go and which steps lead there, with the people it concerns.

  • Role reflection

    Who does what, and who decides what? Clear roles make collaboration easier, especially in growing and self-organised structures.

  • Teams and groups

    We design and facilitate retreats, planning and reflection formats, from small circles to large groups.

  • Conflict clarification

    Tensions become discussable, and you find agreements that everyone can work with.

  • Impact Networks

    In networks, many organisations work on a shared concern. We accompany their set-up, their coordination and their ways of deciding together.

  • Social movements and initiatives

    Volunteer-run and grassroots democratic groups find structures that fit their values, also in the transition from initiative to organisation.

  • Social enterprises

    Societal impact and economic activity belong together here. We accompany organisations that develop both side by side.

  • Change processes

    Growth, reorientation, generational change or new funding. We accompany such transitions so that your organisation stays able to act.

How we work.

For us, systemic means working with hypotheses instead of diagnoses, offering impulses for self-organisation and trusting that, with support, you develop the best solutions yourselves. We look at the interplay of people, structures and the environment in which your organisation works.

For this we bring a wide range of methods and choose according to your needs. They range from participatory workshop formats and co-creative approaches to systemic questions and system maps that make interactions visible. We work with modern methods and keep developing them.

As an institute for Systemic Impact Orientation, we also ask in every process what impact your organisation wants to achieve in society. In this way, organisational development becomes a way to achieve that impact better. Where your organisation wants to be part of a larger change, we connect the work inside with the view on Systems Change. If you want to go deeper into impact, you will find it in our offer Impact Consulting.

What we bring.

We bring the craft of participatory processes. We design planning and reflection formats, accompany teams and individuals over longer periods and coordinate with the leadership, so that the work in the team stays connected to the goals of the organisation.

Stefan brings more than 15 years of designing and accompanying projects in peace work and in social movements. This includes six years with the Civil Peace Service in Cambodia with nearly ten partner organisations, from memorial work and psychosocial services to the university and youth work. It is supported by ongoing training in systemic organisational development.

How an offer comes about.

We see ourselves as an organising actor. Depending on your concern, we bring in further people and develop a solution together with you.

So that we can make you a concrete offer, we first clarify your request together. This initial clarification is free of charge and without obligation. Usually we already develop first approaches in an online conversation on how you can take your concern forward in terms of your impact. Only then do we make you an offer. Write to us.

Services

Impact Up is under construction — and the services are taking shape along with the institute. With our views and perspectives on systemic impact orientation, we want to meet the needs we encounter: among individuals, in organisations, in networks. What we develop is guided above all by what people wish for — so inquiries of every kind are welcome.

There are no ready-made packages here. If you want something, write to us about it — and together we will see whether and how it can be done. Impact Up is a community construct: behind the services stand competencies and a network, and new things emerge from working together. Send a service inquiry

01

Coaching

Coaching for individuals and teams of up to five people. We agree on the direction with the leadership, and gladly with the team itself — and then we work on that direction: with agreements on structure and the proven elements of coaching, so that a team works well. Impact orientation is already at play here, because the direction is the common thread. These settings hold our longest experience.

02

Impact Consulting

Developing · unfolding · connecting impact: these are the three movements of impact consulting. An organisation exists for a goal, and where that goal is a societal impact, consulting begins exactly there. In substance this is organisational consulting; impact consulting is the better word because it says what is being worked on: developing the impact, unfolding it through the organisation, and connecting it with the field and society.

03

Integrating Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence brings change everywhere — including the fields of impact where we want to achieve something. It can help these systems, and we should use that contribution: whoever wants more social participation, more nonviolence, an intact ecosystem, should make sure quickly that artificial intelligence contributes to those goals too — because that determines which direction it takes overall. We integrate it into the work of organisations: tied to their language, their impact logic and their standards, with the experience of a year of intensive practice of our own.

All three services stand on the same foundation: systemic impact orientation. They become more specific the more people use them — and more will follow.

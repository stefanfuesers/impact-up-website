Services

Impact Up is under construction — and the services are evolving along with the institute. What develops here also depends on the energy that emerges here: what you wish for, what you need to connect with one another — your ideas and needs matter to us.

For now there is a small starting offer, developed from our resources, the founding idea of the institute and our ideas for the future. We are glad if it speaks to you — and at least as glad to develop something together with you. Do reach out to us with your ideas and needs — service inquiry.

01

Project Coaching

We developed this offer from our own resources. Impact Up Project Coaching is aimed at project teams and leaders within projects. We accompany you in planning, in regular check-ins during long projects, and just as much through difficult changes and project phases — systemically, with shared clarity of goals and roles as our orientation.

 02

Impact Consulting

We developed this offer from the founding idea of the institute. Impact Up impact consulting looks at your impact as a whole — systemically, starting from your topics: developing impact, unfolding it and connecting it with others, so that coherence and cooperation can grow.

Integrating Artificial Intelligence

We developed this offer from an idea for the future. Integrating Artificial Intelligence at Impact Up uses AI above all to bring your impact into the world — systemically, starting from your topics, with the best ethical AIs.

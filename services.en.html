Services

Impact Up is under construction — and the services are taking shape along with the institute. What we develop is guided by what people wish for. Please write to us with your ideas and needs. We look forward to seeing together how far we can get, and whom we can bring in to realise your ideas and meet your needs — service inquiry.

01

Project Coaching

Project Coaching for individuals and teams of up to five people. We agree on the direction with the leadership, and gladly with the team itself — and then we work on that direction: on clarity of goals and clarity of roles, so that a team works well. These settings hold our longest experience.

Impact Consulting

Developing, unfolding and connecting impact — these are the three movements of impact consulting. An organisation exists for a goal, and where that goal is a societal impact, consulting begins exactly there.

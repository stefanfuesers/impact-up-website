Impact Up Workshop

Workshop

We are a workshop: this is where we build tools you can use directly — to test, to try out, to think further with. The work is done with artificial intelligence. For now, everything is free of charge; that may change over time. The first tool is the Session Builder, more are in the making.

01

Session Builder

A tool in the making

In a few minutes you put together a small session for a concrete matter — from a difficult one-on-one conversation to a two-day retreat. You answer a few questions: what it is about, where you stand, what you want to reach.

Out comes a handful of methods from a base of 190, notes on what to watch for, and two or three links for reading on. A simple mechanism assembles the building blocks — as a first inspiration to think further with. What becomes of it is yours to decide. Everything stays in your browser.

→ Open the Session Builder

Once you have tried the Session Builder and want to tell us what worked well and what is missing — we are glad to listen: info@impact-up.org.

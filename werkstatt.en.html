Tool-Lab

This is the Tool-Lab. We offer you tools that are based on our experience with workshops and process facilitation and that are easier to build with AI today. The Tool-Lab is under construction. You can try all tools free of charge, and they live from your feedback. Write to us if a method is missing or you come across a gap or an error. Extensions that cost money to run, such as AI support or joint live mapping, we will build as soon as there is a need. Most of all, we like to use the tools together with you in your workshops and processes. For enquiries about this, for experiences and feedback, feel free to write to us.

02

Mapping Editor

With the Mapping Editor, you work out a map of your topic as a group. There are three map types to choose from. The Systems Map shows how factors influence each other and where they reinforce or balance one another. The Impact Map shows how your activities are meant to lead through results to the impact you aim for. The Actors Map shows who is involved in a topic and how the actors relate to each other. The editor runs directly in your browser, and your data stays on your own computer.

Session Builder

The Session Builder is a shortcut to a well-prepared workshop or another meeting, a first draft. You answer a few questions, and at the end there is a possible way to shape the time meaningfully and purposefully. The tool offers you 190 methods and many accompanying ideas in a modular system. And of course it remains inspiration: the realisation still has to be done, gladly also by us.

→ Open the Session Builder

With your own AI

Both tools are also open to your own AI. An AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT can draft a map from your documents, which you open in the Mapping Editor and keep working on together. For a session, it uses the 190 methods of the Session Builder as a source. You paste the plan it proposes into the Session Builder and download it as a PDF. Give your AI the link impact-up.org/llms.txt, where it finds everything it needs.

Workshop

We are a workshop: this is where we build tools you can use directly — to test, to try out, to think further with. The first tool is the Session Builder, more are in the making. Feedback from trying things out helps us build — write to us.

01

Session Builder

In a few minutes you put together a small example session for a concrete matter — from a difficult one-on-one conversation to a two-day retreat. You answer a few questions, and out comes a handful of methods from a base of 190, notes on what to watch for, and two or three links for reading on — as a first inspiration. What becomes of it is yours to decide; everything stays in your browser.

Impact Consulting

Impact orientation at Impact Up means aligning your work with the change you want to achieve in society. For this we bring expertise in planning, monitoring and evaluation (PM&E) and work as process consultants with a systemic stance. The impact remains yours. Our special focus is on relationships, because impact in society arises where people and organisations work well together.

What we accompany.

Depending on where you currently stand, it is one or several of these topics.

  • Clarifying impact

    Together you formulate what change you aim for, so that staff, volunteers and partners find themselves in it.

  • Developing Impacts and Outcomes

    The big impact becomes concrete changes that guide your work in everyday life.

  • Theory of Change

    We make visible through which steps and assumptions your work leads to impact, and we test these assumptions together.

  • Outcome Mapping

    The focus is on the people and organisations you work with, on the changes in their actions and on your relationships with them.

  • Monitoring and evaluation

    Indicators and formats that you use in everyday work and from which you learn what your work achieves.

  • Impact-oriented strategy

    Strategy and impact logic are developed together, so that your decisions are aligned with impact.

  • Cooperation in the impact field

    You clarify with whom you work on the same impact and how you strengthen each other. In this way, many single impacts become a coherent field.

  • Impact Networks and Systems Change

    When several actors work together on a change, we accompany their shared direction, for example with the Six Conditions of Systems Change.

How we work.

We start from the four perspectives on impact that we describe on the home page. They range from resonance among the people through the structure of the organisation to coherence in the impact field and cooperation in society. We develop impact together with those who work towards it, until it is consistent within. We unfold it by aligning teams, roles and structures with it. And we connect it with the impact field and the society in which it is meant to change something.

Methodologically we draw on a wide variety. We combine the tools of impact planning with participatory and co-creative formats and with reflection formats in which your experience counts. What we use depends on your needs and on what you can continue on your own.

In society, many impacts interlock. That is why we work in dialogue, with principles and with learning loops. How we bring systemic work and impact orientation together is described under Systemic Impact Orientation. If it is about the development of your organisation itself, you will find that in our offer Systemic Organisational Development.

What we bring.

We shape impact with small initiatives as well as with large networks, and we start where it makes sense for you.

Stefan brings experience from numerous impact plannings, among them six years with the Civil Peace Service in Cambodia. He took part in two larger co-creative strategy developments with many participants.

How a collaboration begins.

We see ourselves as an organising actor. Depending on your concern, we bring in further people and develop a solution together with you.

So that we can make you a concrete offer, we first clarify your request together. This initial clarification is free of charge and without obligation. Usually we already develop first approaches in an online conversation on how you can take your concern forward in terms of your impact. Write to us.
